# Zhixun Zheng

Research, code, and writing, collected in one place.

This repository brings together my independent research projects, computational experiments, and creative writing.
## Research & Computational Projects

### 01. Cross-Domain Time-Series Research

I started by asking whether prediction errors from unrelated domains could move together. That led to a different question: how often do lag-scan tests find relationships that aren't actually there?

- [Research Paper & Reproducibility Code](https://github.com/ricardo05143018-creator/lag-optimized-time-series-tests)
- [Cross-Domain Error Dynamics — Development History](https://github.com/ricardo05143018-creator/cross-domain-error-dynamics)

### 02. Who Bears the Error? — COMPAS Fairness Audit

I examined 7,214 COMPAS records to see how changing classification thresholds affects error rates and their distribution across groups. The project also tests what happens when different types of mistakes are given different costs.

- [Paper, Code & Results](https://github.com/ricardo05143018-creator/who-bears-the-error-compas)

### 03. Volatility-Aware Backtesting Engine

What began as a moving-average backtest eventually became a portfolio allocation experiment, with an early-2026 holdout and 20,000 simulated scenarios. The later versions examine how much the results depend on the assumptions used.

- [Project Repository & Technical Report](https://github.com/ricardo05143018-creator/volatility-aware-backtesting-engine)

### 04. 2026 World Cup Poisson Model

I built and revised this model during the 2026 World Cup. It started with hand-set team ratings and gradually moved toward estimates fitted from match results. I kept the earlier versions, including the predictions that went wrong.

- [Code & Version History](https://github.com/ricardo05143018-creator/soccer-poisson-model)

## Fiction & Creative Writing

### 05. Orchard Place

A novel of approximately 43,000 words, told across fifteen chapters and an epilogue.

I also built a companion website where readers can explore the story beyond the chapters: an unresolved bus route, its evidence, overlapping timelines, character records, and moments from the novel.

- [Read the Novel & Explore the Archive](https://orchard-place-preview.vercel.app/)

### 06. Eleven Days, Ten Years

I watched ten years of *My Hero Academia* in eleven days, then wrote about what it felt like to experience so much of a story almost all at once.

The resulting literary essay follows what happened to my sense of time, memory, and attachment along the way.

### 07. If Anything Is Wrong

A 16-page experimental story that begins with small corrections to dates and objects. As the corrections accumulate, they start changing entire passages, the chronology, and eventually the relationship at the center of the story.

### 08. Independent Creative Nonfiction

Three essays about different things: a World Cup comeback, a band arrangement, and an attempt to explain code to my mother.
